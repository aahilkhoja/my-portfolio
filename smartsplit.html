SmartSplit App Design

A budgeting app concept that automatically routes a user's paycheck into Spend, Save, and Invest accounts based on a short onboarding flow, covering sign-up through to the home dashboard as a working prototype.

Role UI/UX Design
Tools Figma, Google Flow
Category UI/UX Design
Timeline 3 Weeks

04

Reflection

Working out the onboarding flow was the part I spent the most time on, since asking someone five questions about their income and habits only works if every question earns its place. Getting the split summary at the end to read like an actual recommendation instead of a random number took a few passes.

If I took this further, I would design the empty and error states for the dashboard, since right now every screen assumes the user already has a healthy amount of activity to look at.
