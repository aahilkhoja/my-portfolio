Sherlock Holmes Book Design

An editorial book design project exploring typography, page rhythm, illustration, and a mystery-inspired reading experience.

The Brief

A reimagined edition of Sherlock Holmes needed a page system that made long-form reading feel atmospheric — deliberate type pairing, page rhythm, and illustration that reinforced the mystery genre without overwhelming the text.
