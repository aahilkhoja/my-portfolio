Reflection

Redesigning a site that is famous for being intentionally over the top was a strange challenge, since the goal was not to fix it in the usual sense but to keep its personality while making it easier to actually use. Deciding what counted as charm versus what counted as friction took longer than the redesign work itself.

If I took this further, I would test it against the original site with a few real users, since a lot of my decisions here were based on my own read of the brand rather than outside feedback.
