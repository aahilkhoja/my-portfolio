Brand Campaign Design

Salustiana Meorganica Campaign Development

A brand campaign development project covering booth design, campaign visuals, and in-person brand presence for Salustiana Meorganica.

Role Campaign & Booth Design
Tools Illustrator, Photoshop
Category Brand Campaign Design
Timeline 3 Weeks
01

Campaign Deck

The full deck end to end, from brand strategy through to the booth, merch, and print and social executions.

02

Reflection

Designing for a physical booth instead of a screen or a page was a good reminder that people experience a space differently depending on where they are standing, not just what is printed on it. Getting the signage to still read clearly from a distance took more iteration than the booth layout itself.

If I extended this, I would design a small handout or sample card to go with it, since right now the campaign covers the space well but does not give a visitor anything to actually take home.
