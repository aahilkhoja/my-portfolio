Product / Packaging Design

Shizuka Matcha Packaging

A premium organic tea packaging system built around seasonal flavors, hand-drawn illustration, and a consistent brand language across the full product line.

Role Packaging & Illustration Design
Tools Illustrator, Photoshop
Category Product Design
Timeline 3 Weeks
01 — Overview

The Brief

Shizuka Matcha needed a packaging system that felt premium and calm on the shelf, while still leaving room for a distinct illustration style across each seasonal flavor variant. The goal was a structure flexible enough to scale across a full product line without losing a consistent brand identity.
