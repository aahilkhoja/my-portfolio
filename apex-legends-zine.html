Reflection

Making a zine around a game I actually play was one of the more fun projects to work on, but it also meant I had to be careful not to let my own excitement clutter the layout. Keeping the page rhythm calm while the subject matter is fast and chaotic was the real challenge.

If I kept going with this, I would add a second issue covering a different legend or map, since right now it only really represents one small slice of what the season actually introduced.
