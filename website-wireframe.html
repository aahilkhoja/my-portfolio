Website Wireframe

A website wireframe built and prototyped entirely in Figma. Instead of static screens, this one is shown as a live prototype below, so you can click through the actual flow the same way a visitor would use the site.

Role Wireframe & UX Design
Tools Figma
Category UI/UX Design
Timeline 1 Week
01

Live Prototype

Best viewed in fullscreen. Click the expand icon inside the frame to open it full screen.

02

Reflection

Building this straight into a working prototype instead of stopping at flat wireframes forced me to actually think through what happens when someone clicks something, not just how a screen looks sitting still.

If I took this further, I would run it past a few people who had never seen it before and watch where they got stuck, since I am too close to the flow at this point to notice the parts that are not actually obvious.
