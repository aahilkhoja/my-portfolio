03

Reflection

This was the first time I designed a full shopping flow instead of just a few key screens, and it made me realize how many small decisions go into something as simple as a checkout button. Keeping the app feeling calm across a fairly large product catalog took more restraint than I expected.

If I went further with this, I would design an empty cart state and a couple of error states, since right now the flow only really shows what happens when everything goes right.
