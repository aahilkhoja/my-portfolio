Reflection

This project let me lean fully into a fictional world without worrying about real brand guidelines holding me back, which was a nice break. The hardest part was making the interior tray feel purposeful instead of like an afterthought, since a lot of collector boxes treat the inside as less important than the lid.

If I built this again, I would design one more accessory card just to round out the set, since right now it reads slightly incomplete compared to how full most collector editions actually feel.
